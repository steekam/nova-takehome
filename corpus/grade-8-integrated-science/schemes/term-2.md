# Grade 8 Integrated Science — Term 2 Scheme of Work

Kenya CBC · Junior School · 10 teaching weeks · 50 lessons · five 40-minute lessons per week.

Reference design: KICD, [Integrated Science Grade 8 — July 2024](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RpqZRmeMywbc1Tya0_x-tNJBs75Gy0-v/preview), linked from the [Grade Eight Designs page](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-eight-designs/). KICD assigns suggested lessons by sub-strand; this term placement follows the school's three-term planning assumption. The term continues 1.3, completes 2.1 and 2.2, and begins 2.3.

Outcome references join each sub-strand number to the source's printed letter and link to existing catalogue records. Source locators: table 1.3, printed 5–6 / PDF 17–18; table 2.1, printed 9–10 / PDF 21–22; table 2.2, printed 11–12 / PDF 23–24; table 2.3, printed 13–14 / PDF 25–26. Weekly lesson numbers are local to each week; term periods run from 1 to 50.

### Week 1 · Term periods 1–5 · 1.3 Classes of fire
L1: Retrieve the fire triangle and explain a danger scenario, 1.3(b,d). L2: Match burning-material scenarios to the supplied fire-class guide, 1.3(c). L3: Explain suitable control principles and unsafe responses, 1.3(c). L4: Rehearse reporting a fire and following an evacuation plan through role cards, 1.3(c,d). L5: Check scenario decisions against the school plan and revise them, 1.3(c,d). Teacher models calm reporting and prevents any live-fire practice. Resources: class guide, school plan and scenario cards; offer seated or spoken roles. Evidence: correct reasoning and an ordered safe response. Source: table 1.3, printed 5–6 / PDF 17–18.

### Week 2 · Term periods 6–10 · 1.3 Classes of fire
L1: Inspect a teacher-selected photograph for fire hazards, 1.3(a,d). L2: Develop prevention messages for a classroom, kitchen or outdoor setting, 1.3(c,d). L3: Complete the fire-class poster begun in Term 1, 1.3(c,d). L4: Explain posters to peers and use feedback to improve accuracy, 1.3(b–d). L5: Complete a scenario assessment and correct unsafe choices, 1.3(a–d). Teacher checks each control claim against vetted guidance; no learner handles firefighting equipment. Resources: photographs, posters and class guide. Evidence: useful messages with clear causes, dangers and safe responses. Source: table 1.3, printed 5–6 / PDF 17–18.

### Week 3 · Term periods 11–15 · 2.1 The Cell
L1: Discuss cells as building units of living things, 2.1(e). L2: Identify microscope parts and practise safe handling, 2.1(a). L3: Follow a teacher demonstration to prepare a temporary plant slide, 2.1(a). L4: Observe and draw plant cells, 2.1(a). L5: Label the structures visible and compare drawings with observations, 2.1(a). Teacher supervises slide handling, provides prepared slides or images when microscopes are limited, and excludes learner body samples. Resources: microscope, clean plant material and slides or enlarged images. Evidence: observation drawing with clear labels. Source: table 2.1, printed 9–10 / PDF 21–22.

### Week 4 · Term periods 16–20 · 2.1 The Cell
L1: Observe teacher-provided animal-cell slides or images, 2.1(a). L2: Draw and label visible components, 2.1(a). L3: Use vetted cards to connect each visible component with a function, 2.1(b). L4: Build an annotated plant-cell model, 2.1(a,b). L5: Explain how the model represents and simplifies an actual cell, 2.1(a,b). Teacher checks that learners do not label structures absent from the observed view. Resources: prepared slides/images, card and counters; offer tactile models and oral labels. Evidence: labelled drawing and matched structure–function explanation. Source: table 2.1, printed 9–10 / PDF 21–22.

### Week 5 · Term periods 21–25 · 2.1 The Cell
L1: Compare plant- and animal-cell observations in a two-column record, 2.1(c). L2: Explain common components and their functions, 2.1(b,c). L3: Identify differences supported by the supplied light-microscope views, 2.1(c). L4: Apply component functions to simple cell scenarios, 2.1(b,e). L5: Exchange comparison records and correct unsupported claims, 2.1(b,c). Teacher distinguishes a model's detail from what was observed. Resources: paired cell images and component cards; use large print or described diagrams. Evidence: similarities and differences linked to observations, plus a reason cells matter in living things. Source: table 2.1, printed 9–10 / PDF 21–22.

### Week 6 · Term periods 26–30 · 2.1 The Cell
L1: Read eyepiece and objective-lens magnifications, 2.1(d). L2: Determine total magnification by multiplying lens values, 2.1(d). L3: Compare views at different magnifications using supplied images or the microscope, 2.1(a,d). L4: Complete observation, comparison and magnification stations, 2.1(a–e). L5: Review station evidence and explain the role of cells in organisms, 2.1(e). Teacher models one calculation and checks microscope care. Resources: labelled lens cards, microscope or images and calculation sheets. Evidence: correct total magnifications, labelled observations and a cell-role explanation. Source: table 2.1, printed 9–10 / PDF 21–22.

### Week 7 · Term periods 31–35 · 2.2 Movement of Materials in and out of the cell
L1: Predict movement when coloured liquid meets clear water, 2.2(a). L2: Observe diffusion and record changes over time, 2.2(a,b). L3: Use particle drawings to explain net spreading, 2.2(a). L4: Observe a teacher-prepared plant-material demonstration or its data, 2.2(b). L5: Separate observations from explanations in a short report, 2.2(a,b). Teacher keeps starting conditions comparable and avoids tasting materials. Resources: water, food colouring, plant material and timer; provide written observations or an accessible video record. Evidence: time record, particle model and evidence-based conclusion. Source: table 2.2, printed 11–12 / PDF 23–24.

### Week 8 · Term periods 36–40 · 2.2 Movement of Materials in and out of the cell
L1: Predict changes in equal plant strips placed in water and a salt solution, 2.2(a). L2: Set up a fair comparison and record initial measurements, 2.2(b). L3: Observe and record final measurements or firmness, 2.2(b). L4: Explain the pattern using movement of water, 2.2(a,b). L5: Compare the plant demonstration with a teacher-prepared membrane model, 2.2(a,b). Teacher prepares cutting equipment and supervises clean-up; no solutions are tasted. Resources: equal plant strips, cups and ruler/balance, or a supplied data set. Evidence: fair-test choices, records and an osmosis explanation. Source: table 2.2, printed 11–12 / PDF 23–24.

### Week 9 · Term periods 41–45 · 2.2 Movement of Materials in and out of the cell
L1: Link water movement to plant water uptake, 2.2(c). L2: Use diagrams to explain movement of gases at the lungs, 2.2(c). L3: Discuss material movement during nutrient absorption, 2.2(c). L4: Match everyday biological examples to diffusion or osmosis and justify choices, 2.2(c,d). L5: Design an explanation card showing why the processes sustain living things, 2.2(d). Teacher checks the distinction between movement of particles generally and water across a selectively permeable boundary. Resources: diagrams, role cards and print sources; accept oral explanations. Evidence: correct process–role links. Source: table 2.2, printed 11–12 / PDF 23–24.

### Week 10 · Term periods 46–50 · 2.2 → 2.3
L1: Complete the diffusion/osmosis checkpoint and correct misconceptions, 2.2(a–d). L2: Establish respectful discussion rules and use an anonymous question box, 2.3(a,f). L3: Sequence a simplified menstrual-cycle chart, 2.3(a). L4: Interpret different cycle-pattern cards without assuming every cycle is identical, 2.3(a). L5: Identify menstrual challenges in fictional scenarios and list questions for a management plan, 2.3(a,b). Teacher uses age-appropriate sources and never requests personal disclosures. Resources: cycle cards and scenario sheets; provide text and diagram versions. Evidence: sequence and a respectful, practical response. Allocation: one lesson to 2.2 and four to 2.3. Sources: table 2.2, printed 11–12 / PDF 23–24; table 2.3, printed 13–14 / PDF 25–26.

Term allocation check: 1.3 = 10 lessons; 2.1 = 20; 2.2 = 16; 2.3 = 4. Total = 50 lessons = 2,000 minutes. Outcome-code coverage: 1.3(a–d), 2.1(a–e), 2.2(a–d), and an introduction to 2.3(a,b,f). Sub-strand 2.3 continues in Term 3; hormonal control and details of blastocyst formation are outside this sequence.
