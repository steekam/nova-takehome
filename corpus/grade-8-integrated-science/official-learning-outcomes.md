# Integrated Science — Grade 8 learning outcomes

Source: [KICD, Integrated Science Grade 8 — July 2024](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RpqZRmeMywbc1Tya0_x-tNJBs75Gy0-v/preview), listed on the [KICD Grade Eight Designs page](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-eight-designs/).

## Strand 1 — Matter

### Sub-strand 1.1 — Elements and Compounds

- 1.1(a) explain the relationship between an atom, an element, a molecule and a compound. [KICD source: printed pp. 1–2 / PDF pp. 13–14]
- 1.1(b) assign symbols to selected elements. [KICD source: printed pp. 1–2 / PDF pp. 13–14]
- 1.1(c) write word equations to represent reactions of selected elements to form compounds. [KICD source: printed pp. 1–2 / PDF pp. 13–14]
- 1.1(d) outline the applications of common elements in the society. [KICD source: printed pp. 1–2 / PDF pp. 13–14]
- 1.1(e) appreciate the information on packaging labels of commonly consumed substances. [KICD source: printed pp. 1–2 / PDF pp. 13–14]

### Sub-strand 1.2 — Physical and Chemical Changes

- 1.2(a) describe the characteristics of particles in the three states of matter. [KICD source: printed pp. 3–4 / PDF pp. 15–16]
- 1.2(b) explain the effects of impurities on boiling point and melting point of a substance. [KICD source: printed pp. 3–4 / PDF pp. 15–16]
- 1.2(c) distinguish between physical and chemical changes in substances. [KICD source: printed pp. 3–4 / PDF pp. 15–16]
- 1.2(d) outline applications of change of state of matter in day-to-day life. [KICD source: printed pp. 3–4 / PDF pp. 15–16]
- 1.2(e) appreciate the applications of change of state of matter in day-to-day life. [KICD source: printed pp. 3–4 / PDF pp. 15–16]

### Sub-strand 1.3 — Classes of Fire

- 1.3(a) identify causes of fire in nature. [KICD source: printed pp. 5–6 / PDF pp. 17–18]
- 1.3(b) explain the role of the components of fire triangle in the spread of fire. [KICD source: printed pp. 5–6 / PDF pp. 17–18]
- 1.3(c) describe ways of controlling different classes of fires. [KICD source: printed pp. 5–6 / PDF pp. 17–18]
- 1.3(d) acknowledge the dangers of fires in nature. [KICD source: printed pp. 5–6 / PDF pp. 17–18]

## Strand 2 — Living Things and Their Environment

### Sub-strand 2.1 — The Cell

- 2.1(a) describe the structure of plant and animal cells as observed under a light microscope. [KICD source: printed pp. 9–10 / PDF pp. 21–22]
- 2.1(b) describe the functions of components of cells seen under the light microscope. [KICD source: printed pp. 9–10 / PDF pp. 21–22]
- 2.1(c) compare plant and animal cells as observed under a light microscope. [KICD source: printed pp. 9–10 / PDF pp. 21–22]
- 2.1(d) determine the magnification of cells under the light microscope. [KICD source: printed pp. 9–10 / PDF pp. 21–22]
- 2.1(e) appreciate the role of cells in living things. [KICD source: printed pp. 9–10 / PDF pp. 21–22]

### Sub-strand 2.2 — Movement of Materials in and out of the Cell

- 2.2(a) outline the process of diffusion and osmosis in cells. [KICD source: printed pp. 11–12 / PDF pp. 23–24]
- 2.2(b) demonstrate diffusion and osmosis in living things. [KICD source: printed pp. 11–12 / PDF pp. 23–24]
- 2.2(c) explain the role of diffusion and osmosis in living things. [KICD source: printed pp. 11–12 / PDF pp. 23–24]
- 2.2(d) appreciate the importance of diffusion and osmosis in living things. [KICD source: printed pp. 11–12 / PDF pp. 23–24]

### Sub-strand 2.3 — Reproduction in Human Beings

- 2.3(a) outline the menstrual cycle and its related challenges in human beings. [KICD source: printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 25–26]
- 2.3(b) develop a plan to manage challenges related to menstrual cycle in human beings. [KICD source: printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 25–26]
- 2.3(c) describe fertilisation and implantation in human beings. [KICD source: printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 25–26]
- 2.3(d) outline symptoms of STIs in human beings. [KICD source: printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 25–26]
- 2.3(e) explain prevention measures for common STIs in human beings. [KICD source: printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 25–26]
- 2.3(f) appreciate the need for a healthy reproductive system. [KICD source: printed pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 25–26]

## Strand 3 — Energy and Forces

### Sub-strand 3.1 — Transformation of Energy

- 3.1(a) identify forms of energy in nature. [KICD source: printed pp. 16–18 / PDF pp. 28–30]
- 3.1(b) classify energy sources into renewable or non-renewable. [KICD source: printed pp. 16–18 / PDF pp. 28–30]
- 3.1(c) demonstrate simple energy transformations in nature. [KICD source: printed pp. 16–18 / PDF pp. 28–30]
- 3.1(d) describe safety measures associated with energy transformation. [KICD source: printed pp. 16–18 / PDF pp. 28–30]
- 3.1(e) appreciate the applications of energy transformation in day-to-day life. [KICD source: printed pp. 16–18 / PDF pp. 28–30]

### Sub-strand 3.2 — Pressure

- 3.2(a) demonstrate pressure in solids and liquids. [KICD source: printed pp. 19–20 / PDF pp. 31–32]
- 3.2(b) identify applications of pressure in solids and liquids. [KICD source: printed pp. 19–20 / PDF pp. 31–32]
- 3.2(c) appreciate the applications of pressure in solids and liquids. [KICD source: printed pp. 19–20 / PDF pp. 31–32]
