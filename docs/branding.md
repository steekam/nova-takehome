# Product branding

The product is **Curriculum Design Hub**, by Nova Pioneer.

- Logo: Nova Pioneer’s multicolor mark, taken from the header on [novapioneer.com](https://www.novapioneer.com/kenya/).
- Typeface: Montserrat Variable, matching Nova Pioneer’s public website stylesheet. The app bundles the font through Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License.
- Palette: Nova Pioneer navy and blue with the mark’s green, orange, and yellow accents.
