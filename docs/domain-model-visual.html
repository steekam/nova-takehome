04 · lesson-plan content

One selected target becomes one teachable session

Context · fixed by the request

KICD CBC → Grade 8 → Integrated Science → selected official outcome → 40 minutes. Keep the exact curriculum wording and source in the record.

Lesson content · AI drafts, Teacher edits

  • Teacher-facing objective derived from the outcome
  • Materials learners need
  • Timed steps: teacher actions and learner actions
  • A check for learning and expected evidence

Sources · linked to the relevant parts

Objective, activity and check each carry source chunk IDs. The server resolves IDs to approved source titles and locators.

History · outside lesson content

Owner, version, AI or teacher origin, status, generation run and lead decision. The Lead reviews the exact submitted version.

Example · particles in the three states

Grade 8 Integrated Science · 40 minutes · example lesson objective, not an official curriculum quote

By the end, learners canCompare particle spacing and movement in solids, liquids and gases.
Lesson sequence
Time / stepTeacher doesLearners do
5 min · StarterShow particle diagrams.Compare them and predict what differs.
25 min · MainGuide groups as they model particles.Build and explain a model for each state.
10 min · ClosingAsk learners to justify one model.Name evidence about spacing and movement.
Check for understanding“Which model represents a gas? What is your evidence?” Look for an explanation about particle spacing and movement.
Source links: official outcome chunk supports the objective; reviewed teaching-note chunk supports the model activity. Optional 5E label, competency, value or PCI appears only when the lesson actually uses it.
